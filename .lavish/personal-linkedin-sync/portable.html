The user’s story

  1. Sign in to Unlinked

    Use the existing Ideaflow account. A future “Continue with LinkedIn” option belongs in shared identity.

  2. Connect their LinkedIn

    Hosted Auth handles login and provider challenges. Show which profile is connected; disclose retention and publishing before connection.

  3. Get a useful first result

    Index incrementally. Let them search the ready subset, then show a truthful receipt when the initial traversal completes.

  4. Come back to current people

    New connections appear automatically. Keep source dates, private notes, archived history and manual edits.

How it fits the app

Home keeps network search first. A compact card explains connection status and freshness.

People shows one person with their source observations. LinkedIn and native Unlinked connections remain distinguishable.

Settings → Connected accounts handles pause, reconnect, disconnect and source-data removal alongside imports.

Agents read the same authorized updated network. Linking a source does not grant sending or invitation permissions.

Messaging retains current Unlinked/OpenChat behavior. A future LinkedIn channel needs an explicit sender identity.

Shared Unipile workspace limits still need measurement. Separate app queues cannot guarantee that the provider has no shared capacity constraint. Inigo’s current live status was not tested in this audit.

“Current” needs a precise promise

New connections: automatic updates, with provider delay. Unipile documents that new-relation notifications can take up to eight hours.

Removed connections: conservative full reconciliation. A failed or incomplete page never removes contacts.

Titles and companies: a separate paced enrichment policy. Checking connection membership does not refresh every profile.

Build in three slices

  1. Durable linkage and isolation

    Two owners, verified callbacks, restart-safe state, Inigo invariant checks.

  2. Network import and updates

    Pagination, indexing, replay handling, reconnect catch-up and deletion coverage.

  3. App integration and acceptance

    Home, People, Settings and agents; notes preserved; mobile and keyboard states.

True LinkedIn social sign-in is a separate identity-service slice. Live account-owner challenges and provider entitlement are real rollout gates.
