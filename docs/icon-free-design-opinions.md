# Icon-free design opinions

Jacob requested independent Gemini and Astra ideas on 2026-10-04 after rejecting the basic-page icon. Both received the professional-network browsing requirements, existing design language and membership/depth distinction, without private person data.

## Astra

Astra recommends framing the cue as what someone can learn by opening a page rather than classifying profiles as complete/incomplete.

1. **Substantive preview:** add one useful line to rich entries, such as previous roles or an About excerpt. Keep identity and link treatment consistent for everyone. Use company descriptions or locations for organizations. This survives alphabetical sorting and conveys substance directly.
2. **Section inventory:** About · Experience · Education lists actual available sections, providing predictable scanning. Do not count the current role already visible in the row as additional experience.
3. **Contextual secondary link:** View background or Read company overview advertises a specific reason to open a richer entry. Reserve duplicate navigation for roomier views.
4. **Grouping:** More profile information / Other connections communicates depth once in richer-first order. Combine with a light preview since grouping disappears in alphabetical order.
5. **Optional destination previews:** useful for inline company names that cannot support a descriptive subline. Keep ordinary navigation intact; do not make first-tap behaviour surprising or make the information hover-only.

**Astra’s preference:** one substantive preview. **Alternative:** available-section inventory. Do not decorate every inline company mention or conflate Imported membership labels with information depth.

## Gemini

Gemini 2.5 Pro responded through Vertex using the configured service account after the earlier user login expired.

1. **Typographic contrast:** keep names readable but vary secondary-text opacity between rich and sparse records. Gemini ranked this first for subtlety.
2. **Avatar ring:** add a thin indigo ring to richer person avatars or company logos. Gemini ranked this second.
3. **Content snippet:** preview an About excerpt for richer profiles, while sparse profiles retain role/company text.
4. **Leading bar:** use a thin muted vertical accent on richer rows.

Gemini agrees with positive signals for additional information and keeping membership separate. Its visual proposals work in alphabetical order and need no hover, but rings and bars do not distinguish ordinary inline company links.

## Assessment

Prefer Astra’s substantive previews or section inventories. Content snippets also appear in Gemini’s ideas, though they are not its first choice. Keep the existing role/company identity line, then add one useful preview rather than replacing professional context. Show a prior role or relevant factual excerpt, not generic copy.

Avoid opacity changes that make useful information less readable. Avatar rings can suggest stories or verification, while leading bars can suggest unread or selected rows; neither has an inherent page-depth meaning. The subtle Details label remains an alternative for compact inline links where a preview cannot fit. No production change is selected by these opinions.
